Publication standard

Evidence before accusation.

  1. 01

    Identify the exact outlet

    A chain name without a verifiable branch or location stays unpublished.

  2. 02

    Use attributable sources

    Official material is preferred. Reputable reporting must name the outlet and attribute the finding.

  3. 03

    Keep findings dated

    Inspection findings are presented as historical events, never as permanent labels.

  4. 04

    Leave a correction trail

    Later responses or orders can update status while the original source remains visible.
