How this works

Food Safety Watch is a sourced index of dated food-safety inspection events in Bengaluru. It is not a current safety rating or permanent blacklist.

What gets published

A record needs an identifiable outlet, an inspection date, an attributable finding or action, and at least one accessible official or reputable news source. Chain-level mentions without a verifiable branch stay unpublished.

How sources are handled

Each factual record links to its reporting sources. Map locations link separately to OpenStreetMap. Business responses and later status updates are retained alongside the original event.

How to read a record

Findings and actions describe what a source reported on a specific date. They do not establish an outlet's present condition. Where a later outcome is unavailable, the record says so directly.

Corrections

If a record identifies the wrong outlet, omits a response, or has a newer official outcome, send the supporting material. Corrections should preserve the source trail rather than silently rewriting history.
